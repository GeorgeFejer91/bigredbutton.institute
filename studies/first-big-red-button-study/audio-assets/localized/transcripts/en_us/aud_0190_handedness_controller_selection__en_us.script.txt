Handedness matters because the hand doing the task can matter ... especially with a controller and the Big Red Button.

There are many other questionnaires that assess handedness more systematically.

Some are not tiny.

The Edinburgh Handedness Inventory is often used as a 10-item scale. The Annett Hand Preference Questionnaire uses 12 items. The Waterloo Handedness Questionnaire has a 32-item version.

They ask about concrete everyday acts: writing, throwing, and using scissors.

Here, in questionnaire mercy ... we ask directly. Please choose the left or right controller you will use for the experiment, because yes ... even that may subtly affect Big Red Button behavior.

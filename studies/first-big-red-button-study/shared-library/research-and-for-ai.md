## Current Research Threads

- Predictive hand-contact preload is allowed for feel only.
- Predictive preload alone must not count as a press.
- `controller_contact` remains the final validation proof.
- `hand_contact` remains supplemental, paper-relevant evidence.
- Force and pressure fields are model-derived proxies, not measured hand force.

## Unity Knowledge Boundary

The Unity subtree is useful for comparison, model assets, glow behavior,
Morphospace/Manifold contracts, and app-owned Quest interaction patterns. It is
not the canonical source for the native study's export schema, final validation
proof, or participant-facing language catalog.

## Consensus And Publication Notes

Consensus search material and HCI literature review assets should remain under
the native `For-AI` folder until the manuscript is separated into a standalone
publication package. Shared references can be mirrored here only as indexes that
point back to the canonical files.

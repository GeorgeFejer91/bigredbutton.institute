# Psychometrics Note

The app uses an adapted Igroup Presence Questionnaire (IPQ) item set for the post-condition presence questionnaire.

## Rationale

Common VR presence instruments include the Witmer-Singer Presence Questionnaire (PQ), the Slater-Usoh-Steed (SUS) presence questionnaire, and the Igroup Presence Questionnaire (IPQ). For this study, IPQ is the best fit because its published structure separates:

- spatial presence
- involvement
- experienced realism
- one general presence item

Those dimensions map cleanly to the experiment's question: how present and object-like the Big Red Button felt after each audio condition.

## Source Boundary

The participant-facing items in this app are reworded to refer to the Big Red Button. That makes this an adapted IPQ-style questionnaire, not a fully validated unmodified instrument. Methods text should say that the study used an adapted presence questionnaire based on the IPQ and should not claim that the adapted wording has independent validation.

Useful sources:

- IPQ official overview and item resources: https://www.igroup.org/pq/ipq/
- IPQ development reference: Schubert, Friedmann, & Regenbrecht (2001), *Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments*, 10(3), 266-281.
- Presence questionnaire review context: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7096541/

## Notes for Use

- Prefer one quote per subsection at most.
- Use quotes as epigraphic seasoning, not evidence substitutes.
- Do not overuse Trump and Kim. The paper becomes louder but not necessarily smarter.
- If a quote enters `Introduction.tex`, add a formal BibTeX entry for the source in `references.bib`.
- Keep all quote integration in the paper's academic voice: serious frame, dry delivery, minimal flourish.

## Evidence Notes

- Pantoliano supports the argument that popular play can normalize nuclear weapons as rewards, success conditions, and symbols of power while obscuring harm.
- Prabhakaran et al. support the broader claim that metaphor is not ornament in political discourse; it changes attention, engagement, and framing.
- Brathen and Herstad support the claim that digital buttons retain familiar physical cues while changing the underlying causal relation from circuit state to computational input and output.
- Myers and Shneiderman support the HCI genealogy linking direct manipulation, visible objects, widgets, toolkits, and interface builders to the ordinary software button.
